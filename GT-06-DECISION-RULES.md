# GT-06 - Fixed Facts, Open Decisions, and Assumption Rules

## Fixed Facts - Don't Change These

The following facts are immutable case context:

- SKU costs, MSRPs, lead times, MOQs, and suppliers as listed.
- Denver launches with four SKUs. The other four are catalog-only.
- The Denver store is 14 people, structured as described in `GT-02-OPENING-STAFFING`. Seven seats are still open.
- Marketing ROI per $1 spent:

| Channel | ROI per $1 spent |
|---|---:|
| Instagram | $6.20 |
| Google | $5.10 |
| Email | $4.00 |
| Facebook | $3.00 |

- Austin survey scores are fixed as listed in `GT-03-COMPANY-MARKET`.
- GreenThreads is Series B, has approximately $40M in revenue, and is not yet profitable.
- Competitors are REI, Patagonia, and regional sustainable brands.
- The Marcus/Jennifer tension is structural. Don't resolve it - work with it.

## Open - You Decide, But Say So

The following are open for teams to decide:

- Company history before the Series B.
- The org chart below Marcus and Jennifer.
- Price positioning against REI and Patagonia.
- Rent and which Denver neighborhood.
- What "success" means beyond opening on time.
- Anything the data doesn't cover.

## Governing Rule

An assumption you state is analysis. An assumption you hide is a hole. Marcus won't notice. Jennifer will.

When using this chunk:

1. Preserve every fixed fact.
2. Label every open assumption explicitly.
3. Distinguish source facts, calculations, interpretations, and assumptions.
4. Do not allow a dataset anomaly to silently rewrite fixed case context.
