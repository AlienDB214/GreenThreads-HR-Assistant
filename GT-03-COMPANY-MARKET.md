# GT-03 - Company Profile and Denver Market

## Company Profile

GreenThreads sells sustainable, eco-friendly apparel in stores and online.

- 12 stores. Denver is #13. The recent run: Seattle -> Portland -> Austin.
- Series B, approximately $40M revenue, not yet profitable. Growth is funded, but every dollar gets watched.
- Two channels: retail and e-commerce.
- Four marketing channels: Instagram, Google Ads, email, Facebook.
- Customers are environmentally conscious.
- Competitors are REI and Patagonia, both deeply established in Denver, plus regional sustainable brands.

## Denver Market

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Metro population | Approximately 3.08M across approximately 1.29M households |
| Median household income | $108,516 - well above national |
| Median age | 35.3 |
| Bachelor's degree or higher | Approximately half the metro |
| Retail vacancy | 4.4% - very tight, almost no new construction |

Colorado is a top-three outdoor apparel state. Nationally, sustainably marketed products are 24.8% of retail spending, eco-shoppers pay a 26.6% premium, and 48% of interested consumers say they can't find sustainable brands. That last number is the opening.
